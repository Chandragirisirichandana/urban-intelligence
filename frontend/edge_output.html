END-TO-END WORKFLOW

From Bus Camera to Municipal Action

How raw optical feeds are transformed into prioritized, actionable municipal dispatches in seconds.

01

Capture

Onboard 1080p cameras continuously scan roadway, traffic lane, and pedestrian crossways during regular transit routes.

02

Edge Analyze

Local edge units execute YOLOv8s computer vision at 22 FPS to detect defects, anomalies, and safety violations.

03

Geotag & Deduplicate

Events are GPS tagged and spatially clustered. Multi-bus sightings increment confidence and filter false positives.

04

Prioritize & Alert

Rule engine generates severity-ranked alerts (Critical, High, Medium) with time-to-collision and visual crop evidence.

05

Municipal Action

Integrated dashboard dispatches repair tasks to GHMC engineering divisions and traffic incident reports to police.

ENGINEERED FOR SCALE

Edge-First Computing.
Zero Bandwidth Saturation.

Traditional smart city solutions require streaming thousands of gigabytes of raw video over 4G/5G networks, creating prohibitive cellular costs. Urban Intelligence executes inference directly on the bus, transmitting only lightweight metadata packets.

98.4% Cellular Bandwidth Reduction
Video stays on the edge. Only JSON telemetry and small cropped evidence bounding boxes leave the vehicle.
Spatial Deduplication Algorithm
Clusters sightings within 15-meter geofences across multiple bus trips to eliminate duplicate municipal tickets.
Offline Resilient Telemetry Queue
Local SQLite cache guarantees zero telemetry loss during tunnel transit or temporary cellular blind spots.
